# From nothing to a decision in twenty minutes

A tutorial: build a plan, read the answer, find what matters most, and keep the change
worth making. It uses only the quick-start wizard and the dashboard; everything it creates
can be refined later.

## 1. Build a plan (five minutes)

Open **Plan → Quick start**. Six short steps ask for ages, pay and pensions, what you have
saved (grouped by how it is taxed), what you spend, and how the money is invested. Rough
figures are fine - the point of the first answer is to see where you stand, not to be
exact.

!!! tip "Save it as a new scenario"
    On the review step, choose **Save as a new scenario**. Nothing you had is changed, and
    you can compare the two later.

## 2. Read the answer (two minutes)

The dashboard opens on your plan. Before any simulation, the top card shows what happens
if markets deliver exactly their typical growth every year - a useful sanity check, never
a forecast. Choose **2,000 trials** and press **Run simulation**. The card now shows the
chance that the money lasts, with its error bar, against your confidence target.

The tiles beneath are the details: when the money runs out at typical growth, the funded
ratio, the median and bad-luck final wealth, first-year spending and lifetime tax.

## 3. Find your biggest levers (one minute)

In **Your biggest levers**, press **Find my levers**. RetPlan tries each common change on
its own - retire a year or two later, spend 5% or 10% less, save more, shift the mix, cut
fees, claim a pension later - and ranks them. Faded rows are within the noise: do not act
on them.

## 4. Combine, and keep (five minutes)

Click the top lever: it loads into the **What if…** sliders. Add a second change - say,
spend 5% less *and* retire a year later - and watch the odds, median wealth and lifetime
tax move. When a combination is worth keeping, press **Save as scenario**.

!!! note "Why the numbers barely wobble"
    Every what-if run uses the same random seed, so the same plan always gives the same
    answer, and two plans differ only because of what you changed.

## 5. Compare and print (five minutes)

Open **Results → Compare scenarios** and press **Simulate all**: every version side by
side on the same trials and seed. Then **Results → Plan report** puts the one you chose on
a single page to print or save as a PDF.

## Where to go next

- **Plan → When to claim a pension** if a public pension is part of the picture.
- **Plan → Conversion explorer** if you hold both taxed-on-the-way-out and tax-free accounts.
- **Portfolio → Build from a spreadsheet** to replace rough balances with your real holdings.
