Market regimes

A Markov chain over market states. The mean offset shifts every asset in proportion to its volatility, the multiplier scales it, and "correlation tightening" pulls everything toward moving as one - which is what actually happens in a crisis.

Crashes

A jump process independent of the regimes, so a crash can strike in any of them. Depth is drawn from a triangular distribution and spread over the chosen number of years; part of the fall is then recovered.
