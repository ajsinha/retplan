How RetPlan compares

Where RetPlan sits among retirement planners and money tools - and what it does that the others do not, and the other way round.

About RetPlan Help

Four families of product touch retirement planning, and each is good at what it does. Subscription planners, such as Boldin (formerly NewRetirement), ProjectionLab and WealthTrace, are polished, quick to start and deep on the rules of the country they are built for. Aggregators, such as Empower, link your accounts and show everything in one place, with a retirement estimate on top. Calculators, such as FIRECalc, cFIREsim and FI Calc, test a withdrawal rate against history, openly and for free. Portfolio tools, such as Portfolio Visualizer and Ghostfolio, analyse holdings but not the life around them.

RetPlan's aim is to be the one you can check: every assumption editable, every number traceable, the odds stated with their precision - and then to help you decide, not just score you.
