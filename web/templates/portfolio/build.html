What it understands
  • A title block above the table - the header row is found wherever it is
  • Headers in broker language: Ticker Symbol, Description, Qty held, Market Value (USD), Avg Cost, Cost Basis, Account Name…
  • Symbols written NASDAQ:AAPL, AAPL US Equity or Apple Inc (AAPL)
  • No symbol at all - an ISIN, CUSIP or just the fund's name is looked up
  • No quantity - worked out from market value and price
  • Cost per share or in total - told apart by size
  • Cash, sweep and money-market lines become cash
  • Totals and subtotals are skipped; repeated lines are merged
  • A price that disagrees with Yahoo's (wrong share class, pence versus pounds) is flagged

The file itself is not kept - only the proposed holdings, until you confirm or discard them.
