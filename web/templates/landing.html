Retirement planning you can audit

RetPlan projects a household's income, spending, debt, tax and investments year by year, then stress-tests the plan against thousands of simulated market histories. Nothing is country-specific: tax is a table of bands you type, and account types are wrappers that say when money is taxed.

Open the dashboard Edit the plan How it works
Tax that inverts exactly

An allowance is a 0% band; a taper is a band with the higher effective rate. Keeping the schedule piecewise linear means "how much must I withdraw to spend X after tax" is solved in one pass, not by a circular reference.

Markets in three layers

A Markov regime (bear / normal / bull), correlated shocks that can be fat-tailed, and a separate jump process for crashes with per-asset transmission - so bonds can gain while equities fall.

Checked, not asserted

The same engine backs a LibreOffice workbook whose formulas agree with it to 7×10-12 over 61 years. Every page carries an audit view and a table twin for every chart.

A worked sample household is loaded so you can look around immediately. Educational model — not financial advice.
