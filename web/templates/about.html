What is RetPlan?

A retirement planner and portfolio tracker you run yourself. It projects income, spending, debt, tax and investments year by year, then tests the plan against thousands of simulated market histories. Alongside the plan, it tracks real portfolios - priced every day from Yahoo Finance - and projects them ten or more years ahead with correlated Monte Carlo, bootstrap and fat-tailed models.

It assumes no country. Tax is a table of bands you type; account types are wrappers that say when money is taxed - in, while it grows, or on the way out. Every plan carries a reconciliation audit that proves money in equals money out in every year of the projection.

In this release

  • MAYA's design adopted: themes, mega-menu navigation, help and about
  • A database (SQLite or PostgreSQL, switchable in config/retplan.toml)
  • Named scenarios and a side-by-side comparison
  • The quick-start wizard; advanced editor columns tucked away
  • Portfolios with daily prices, FX, risk checks, rebalancing and projections
  • Historical crisis replays and sequence-risk projections

Honest limits

  • Household-level tax. A couple's income is taxed as one unit, which overstates tax where individuals are taxed separately and incomes are uneven.
  • Annual periods in the plan; within-year timing is not modelled. Portfolio projections run quarterly.
  • Capital gains are the gain share of a withdrawal times an inclusion rate, under the ordinary bands.
  • One year of prices estimates volatility and correlation well and the mean hardly at all, so expected returns lean on long-run assumptions.
  • Crisis replays use rounded index approximations, not a precise record.
  • Yahoo's endpoints are unofficial and may change or throttle.
  • Not financial advice. Projections under assumptions you supply, not predictions.

Privacy and licence

Plans and portfolios live in your own database, keyed by an opaque workspace id in your browser's session cookie; there are no accounts and no tracking. The only outbound traffic is price and symbol lookups to Yahoo Finance.
