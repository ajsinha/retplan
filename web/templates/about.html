About {{ app_name }}

A household cash-flow and portfolio model, version {{ app_version }}.

What it does

Projects income, spending, debt, tax and investments year by year, then stress-tests the plan against thousands of simulated market histories. The same engine drives a LibreOffice workbook whose formulas are proved to agree with it to 7×10-12 over 61 years.

Known limitations — please read
  • Household-level tax. Income is taxed as one unit rather than split between two people with separate bands. Where a jurisdiction taxes individuals separately this overstates tax for couples with uneven incomes.
  • Annual periods. Within-year timing of flows is not modelled.
  • Allowances are bands. A tax-free allowance is entered as a 0% first band and a taper as a band with the higher effective rate.
  • Capital gains are taxed as the gain share of a withdrawal times an inclusion rate, under the ordinary bands. No separate gains allowance.
  • Survivor mechanics are simplified — continuation percentages apply; account consolidation is not modelled in full.
  • Long-term care, annuity purchase and wrapper conversions are not yet modelled as their own modules. Care costs can be entered today as an expense row with a probability, which is what the sample does.
Privacy

Your plan is stored on this server as JSON, keyed by an opaque id in your session cookie. No personal identifier beyond a display name is needed, and nothing is sent anywhere else. Download your plan at any time from the editor.

Not financial advice

This is an educational model. Its outputs are projections under assumptions you supply, not predictions, and must not be relied on as financial, tax or investment advice.
