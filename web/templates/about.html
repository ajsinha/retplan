What is RetPlan?

RetPlan is a retirement planner and portfolio tracker you run yourself. It projects a household's income, spending, debt, tax and investments year by year, tests the plan against thousands of market histories, and tracks real portfolios, priced every day, projected ten or more years ahead.

It assumes no country. Tax is a table of bands you type; accounts are wrappers that say when money is taxed - on the way in, while it grows, or on the way out - so a 401(k), an ISA, a TFSA and a Roth are all one model. And it keeps its own books: every plan carries a reconciliation proving that money in equals money out, in every year of every simulated future.

Built for people who want to understand their retirement, not be sold one: the careful do-it-yourself planner, the family member everyone asks, the adviser who wants to show the working.

Honest limits

Stated so nobody has to discover them.

  • A couple's income is taxed as one unit - too much tax where individuals are taxed separately and incomes differ.
  • The plan runs in whole years; portfolio projections in quarters.
  • No country's special rules: social-security benefit formulas, Medicare, conversion limits and waiting periods are yours to enter or check.
  • No bank or broker linking: holdings come in by typing, pasting or uploading.
  • A year of prices estimates volatility and correlation well and the mean hardly at all, so expected returns lean on long-run assumptions.
  • Crisis replays use rounded index figures; Yahoo's endpoints are unofficial and can throttle.
  • Not financial advice: projections under assumptions you supply.
How it compares

Subscription planners are polished and quick to start, and some know one country's rules in depth. What they rarely do is show their working, let you change every assumption, or leave your data with you. RetPlan does all three - and adds decision tools that treat the answer as something you act on.
